# Attribution and reuse

The catalogue publishes factual metadata, researcher summaries and original-source links. It does not republish sermon text, caption files, manuscript bodies or recordings, and it does not grant permission to collect or reuse those works. Check the original publisher's terms for the intended use. A record's private-retention assessment is not public-release clearance.

Church-location selection derives from **Overture Maps Places release 2026-10-21.0**, including applicable Foursquare and AllThePlaces data. Locations were filtered, normalized, sampled and reviewed. The catalogue's changes and findings are not endorsed by those providers.

The U.S. state map uses the geometry and attribution recorded in `data/catalogue.json`, with the [original ISC notice](notices/us-atlas-ISC.txt). The interactive HTML includes these notices as well.

Publisher names, source titles and linked works remain subject to their respective rights and terms. There is no blanket license here over third-party sermon works. Making this catalogue public does not change those rights.

The collection-feasibility data adds original summaries of publisher indexes, dated item observations, access conditions and license notices. Original links accompany each source and item. No full page, manuscript, caption or recording bodies are included. Creative Commons notices described in source details apply only to identified works and uses; they do not license this entire catalogue or unrelated church material.

The historical roster derives from **Paul Lendway (2026), The Political Ramifications of Sermon Rhetoric among White U.S. Protestants and White Catholics, Yale Dataverse, V2**: [dataset DOI](https://doi.org/10.60600/YU/C1PIEY), [Tally file 42422, version 2.0](https://dataverse.yale.edu/file.xhtml?fileId=42422&version=2.0). Selected factual metadata were grouped by normalized name, county and state. The dataset’s CC0 notice applies only to rights the depositor can waive; it does not clear third-party sermons or imply endorsement. No respondent data are published.

Holy Trinity annual metadata come from its [official sermon archive](https://www.holytrinitysf.org/holy-trinity-sermons), checked 6 November 2026. The [official parish directory](https://sanfran.goarch.org/parishes/holy-trinity-greek-orthodox-church-san-francisco) supports current name associations, not every historical appointment or event. Audio links are references only; this catalogue acquires or redistributes no recording or transcript.

The published-study notes cite original articles, documentation and the inspected repository. Article licenses and code-license statements do not establish rights to underlying third-party sermon or caption bodies.

The Catholic follow-up preserves original Yale roster names and dates beside current parish and diocesan evidence. The two separate written-homily archives are [St. Thomas More, Irvine](https://stmirvine.org/ministries/deacon/deacon-tonys-homilies/) and [Ave Maria, Florida](https://www.avemariaparish.org/father-vidals-homilies); exact archive and PDF links accompany the metadata. Inspected PDFs are summarized in original research notes, not reproduced. No open license over these manuscripts or embedded third-party passages was established. Clergy roles and dates retain the source-specific qualifications recorded on each detail page.

The comparison-source layer selects original research summaries and factual observations from existing sources. Seattle’s expanded metadata link the [St James event archive](https://www.stjames-cathedral.org/livestreamarchive.aspx), [pastor emeritus writing index](https://www.stjames-cathedral.org/Pubs/Pastor/pastoremeritus.aspx), [pastor writing index](https://www.stjames-cathedral.org/Pubs/Pastor/pastorsdesk.aspx), and exact item pages. Fourth’s service evidence links dated bulletins associated with its [2022](https://www.fourthchurch.org/sermons/2022/index.html) and [2025](https://www.fourthchurch.org/sermons/2025/index.html) indexes. No bulletin prose, sermon text or media is reproduced. Source-selection priority is a research recommendation within this selected set, not a new license or endorsement by the institutions.
